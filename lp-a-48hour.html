Straight answers

The things you're wondering.

Is it actually free, or is there a hidden fee?

Actually free. You pay nothing to have the site built and nothing to look at it. Money only enters the conversation if you decide you want to keep it and put it live on your own domain — and you'll know that number before you commit to anything.

Why won't you put prices on this page?

Because a plumber needing one page and a company needing bookings, quotes and a customer database are completely different jobs. Any number we put here would be wrong for most people. Message us, tell us what you need, and you get a real figure — usually inside the same conversation.

What if I want changes?

Expected. The first round of changes is included in the free build. Tell us what's wrong and we fix it before you decide anything.

Do I need a domain or hosting already?

No. If you have one we'll use it. If you don't, we sort it out when you decide to go live. Nothing to arrange before you message us.

How do I know you'll still be around next year?

You don't, and you shouldn't take anyone's word for it. So we build on standard tools you own — your domain, your hosting account, your Google account. If we vanish tomorrow, your site is still yours and any developer can pick it up. No hostage situations.

I'm not in Cape Town. Can you still help?

Yes. We're Cape Town based but the whole thing runs on WhatsApp, so we build for businesses across South Africa.
