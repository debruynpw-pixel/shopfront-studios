Pattern recognition

What we find on nearly every site.

After enough of these, the same handful of problems show up again and again.

1

The visitor can't tell what you do

Headlines like "Welcome to our website" or "Excellence in service since 2011." A stranger has to work out your business from context — and most won't bother.

Loses the majority of first-time visitors
2

No obvious next step

An email address buried in the footer isn't a call to action. If someone wants to hire you at 9pm on a Sunday, there's nothing for them to press.

Kills the ready-to-buy visitor
3

Painful on a phone

Text you have to pinch to read, buttons too small for a thumb, images that take eight seconds to load on mobile data.

Loses your biggest traffic group
4

Contact form, not conversation

South Africans message. A form that promises a reply "within 48 hours" is asking someone to wait two days for a job they want done now.

Most enquiries never get sent
5

Invisible in local search

No location signals, no Google Business setup, no structure for AI assistants to read. You exist, but only for people who already know your name.

Zero new discovery
6

Nothing that says "trust me"

No reviews, no real photos of real work, no named human. Every hesitant buyer is looking for a reason to believe you, and finds none.
